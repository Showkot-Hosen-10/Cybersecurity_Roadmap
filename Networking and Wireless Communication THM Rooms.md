## Wireless & Networking Rooms (TryHackMe)

1. [Intro to LAN](https://tryhackme.com/room/introtolan)  
   Foundational room on local area networks, IP addressing, subnetting, and how devices communicate at a basic level.

2. [Introductory Networking](https://tryhackme.com/room/introtonetworking)  
   High-level introduction to core networking concepts, OSI model, and common protocols useful before diving into more complex labs.

3. [Network Traffic Basics](https://tryhackme.com/room/networktrafficbasics)  
   Explains how to capture and analyse network traffic, introducing PCAPs and common tools used in network forensics and monitoring.

4. [Network Security Essentials](https://tryhackme.com/room/networksecurityessentials)  
   Covers network perimeters, firewalls, and key threats, with practical log review to spot suspicious activity on networks.

5. [Nmap Live Host Discovery](https://tryhackme.com/room/nmap01)  
   Focused room on discovering live hosts using Nmap (ARP, ICMP, TCP/UDP ping scans) as a basis for later network enumeration.

6. [Network Services](https://tryhackme.com/room/networkservices)  
   Teaches enumeration and exploitation of common network services (e.g., FTP, SSH, SMB) and their misconfigurations.

7. [Network Services 2](https://tryhackme.com/room/networkservices2)  
   Sequel room introducing more complex and less common network services and how to identify and exploit their weaknesses.

8. [Wifi Hacking 101](https://tryhackme.com/room/wifihacking101)  
   Practical wireless security room covering WPA/WPA2 attacks, handshake capture, and cracking against Wi-Fi networks.

9. [Ice](https://tryhackme.com/room/ice)  
   CTF-style Windows box that reinforces network enumeration, service discovery and exploitation over a routed environment.

10. [h4cked](https://tryhackme.com/room/h4cked)  
    PCAP-focused room where you analyse captured traffic to reconstruct an attack and understand how the network was compromised.

11. [Carnage](https://tryhackme.com/room/c2carnage)  
    Intermediate packet analysis lab digging into malicious traffic patterns and incident reconstruction from PCAP files.

12. [CCT2019](https://tryhackme.com/room/cct2019)  
    Challenge room that relies heavily on interpreting network captures and understanding protocols to extract flags.

13. [Overpass 2 - Hacked](https://tryhackme.com/room/overpass2hacked)  
    Incident-response style room focused on log and network capture analysis to understand how an environment was breached.
