# TryHackMe Multimedia & Steganography Rooms

This section focuses on **image and file-based steganography**, as well as multimedia-themed CTFs where extracting hidden data from pictures, audio, or other media is a primary skill. These rooms are great for building a stego toolkit (strings, exiftool, steghide, zsteg, binwalk, etc.) and pattern recognition for hidden data.

## Core Steganography Rooms

1. **CC: Steganography** – `https://tryhackme.com/room/ccsteganography`  
   Dedicated introduction to classical steganography challenges involving images and files, covering common hiding techniques and basic stego tooling. [web:1]

2. **Musical Stego** – `https://tryhackme.com/room/musicalstego`  
   Multimedia-focused stego room where audio files and music-related artefacts hide flags or secrets using encoding tricks. [web:1]

3. **Madness** – `https://tryhackme.com/room/madness`  
   CTF-style box that leans heavily on multi-layer steganography in images and other artefacts to progressively recover credentials and flags. [web:1]

4. **Psycho Break** – `https://tryhackme.com/room/psychobreak`  
   Challenge with strong focus on hidden data inside media/content, mixing steganography with light enumeration and basic exploitation. [web:1]

5. **Unstable Twin** – `https://tryhackme.com/room/unstabletwin`  
   Stego-centric room where twin or “paired” artefacts must be compared to uncover embedded messages or anomalies. [web:1]

6. **Cicada-3301 Vol:1** – `https://tryhackme.com/room/cicada3301vol1`  
   Puzzle-heavy room inspired by the Cicada 3301 style, using images and encoded data that often require stego and multimedia analysis to solve. [web:1]

## CTFs with Significant Stego Components

These boxes are not purely “steganography” rooms but contain notable stego/media challenges as part of the path to root.

1. **Cat Pictures** – `https://tryhackme.com/room/catpictures`  
   Web and file-analysis CTF where images and media play a role in hiding or hinting at sensitive information. [web:1]

2. **Madness** (revisited as harder stego set) – `https://tryhackme.com/room/madness`  
   When revisited with more experience, this room serves as a tougher multi-stage stego lab built around layered encodings. [web:1]

3. **Psycho Break** (advanced stego portions) – `https://tryhackme.com/room/psychobreak`  
   Later stages require chaining multiple stego tools and transformations to fully extract hidden content. [web:1]
